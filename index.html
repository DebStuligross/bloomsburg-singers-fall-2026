Concert Highlights

In his November 23 note, Joe thanked the singers for their musical commitment and recalled a few moments from Sunday's concert:

Full choir with orchestra

Magnificat

Joe especially praised the rhythmic energy of Durante’s Magnificat and the excitement of making music with the orchestra.

Expressive contrasts

Te Deum

He remembered the quiet pleading in “quos pretiosos” and the thrilling, unexpectedly fast conclusion of Jenkins’s work.

Intimate music

Chamber Choir

Joe described the chamber performances as especially sensitive, beautiful, and energized — the ensemble’s strongest performances of these works.

Joe also expressed appreciation for the instrumentalists, guest singers, soloists, section leaders, volunteers, and everyone who helped make the performance possible. He welcomes hearing which moments stood out to singers and their friends and families.

Looking ahead: Joe expects rehearsals for the Spring 2027 season to begin in March. Details will follow in the New Year.
