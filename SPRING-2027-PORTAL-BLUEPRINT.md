## Expected layout and reading order

1. **Header:** Bloomsburg Singers / Singer Portal / season, concert title and subtitle, confirmed concert time/place, highlighted NEXT rehearsal callout. Artistic Director **Joe Stuligross** and `jstuligross@gmail.com` visible near the top.
2. **Sticky navigation** reflecting the actual order of the sections.
3. **Singer resources banner:** subtle music-folder photograph, readable overlay, one clear sentence about the site's purpose.
4. **Upcoming Activities / Schedule:** prominent responsive **three-card layout** above checklist when it helps convey the most time-sensitive rehearsals, calls, or performances. Use the same restrained colors and typography, but substitute **confirmed spring dates and times**.
5. **Singer Checklist:** directly below schedule, with only the next rehearsal's music, priorities, measures, section details, and specific instructions from Joe. Replace or remove stale items each week; no backlog of old practice notes in the active checklist.
6. **Concert Repertoire:** ensemble grouping and piece/composer names, links to legitimate model recordings and part-rehearsal tracks by voice. Include John Fletcher Music signup and access notes only if the group/link is valid for spring. Never assume Fall links, pieces, or solos carry forward.
7. **Rehearsal Logistics:** locations, normal and chamber times, attendance, dress rehearsal plan, folders, fragrance, and relevant confirmed procedures.
8. **Other Singing Opportunities & Events:** future dates and singer-facing external invitations, with a named contact when available.
9. **Concert Dress:** toward the lower part of the page until performance week; confirm new season details.
10. **Tuition and Assistance:** lower-page information; verify new fees, exemptions, and volunteer arrangements before reusing any Fall amounts.
11. **Email Digest:** newest at top, prior email body archived separately from the summarized current checklist. Preserve original message meaning and historical notes without silently changing past dates and claims.
12. **Footer:** bump version number and "Last updated" date after each material site edit.
